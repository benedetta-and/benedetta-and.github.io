reasons for the existence of a personal html blog, given it’s 2025 and people have jobs

october 16, 2025

My body, like my personality, is pretty resistant to change. No matter how dark I dye my hair it reverts to this light red color it has chosen as its standard in the past two years. It’s like it has decided that a shade around 6.43 on the dye box scale is its face, and this whatever wavy-straight-curly pattern is its shape. No hairdresser can change that. In the continuous push to become a straight line that goes up, I can’t help but wiggle out just a little bit and to vaguely disagree with the backdrop of my little act. “Just a little bit” and “vaguely” here clearly mean I’m still as dissentient as it is acceptable and as right in my place as I should be. In trying to change that, I get lost in the possibilities in the side projects in the surviving aspects of the life we live.

This is to say that this space exists as a further removal from the shape I feel compelled to fill and as a further abandonment to this 6.43 hair colour and to this semi-wavy pattern. In other words: I’m sick of logging into instagram and watching stories that have been looking the same for the past ten years and it feels too silly to pretend to want to act relevantly there. This is much more my style as I can be as creative as I want and nobody will most-likely read anything I have to say, so I can hold a position without holding a megaphone. I am extremely sensitive to noise.

I also am as nostalgic as anyone in my generation and thus I feel like I have to recreate the visual conditions that welcomed me to the space of the internet some seventeen years ago when I used the web to play girls games and to browse animal wikipedia pages. This is my attempt to have an artisanal and understated presence on the internet, just like the place I would love to have in real life.

This was my favourite wikipedia entry when I was nine: wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox,

but then it changed to a more alternative one:wikipedia.org/wiki/Ocelot

It's important to remember these things.
